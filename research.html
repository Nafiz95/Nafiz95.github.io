Research & Publication

My current work asks how medical vision-language systems represent clinical information, perform on rare findings, and support trustworthy interpretation and evaluation.

Browse publications
01 · Medical Vision-Language Models

Understanding multimodal models in radiology

I study how multimodal foundation models represent radiology images and clinical concepts. CTVLM-Inspect evaluates chest-CT vision-language models across classification, calibration, retrieval, reconstruction consistency, and interpretability.

Current questions

  • What clinical information do chest-CT vision-language models represent reliably?
  • How do classification, retrieval, calibration, and reconstruction-consistency evaluations complement one another?
  • Where do model behaviour and clinical concepts diverge across tasks and datasets?
02 · Long-Tailed Medical AI

Making rare findings visible in evaluation

I study rare-finding recognition, cross-dataset behaviour, calibration, grounding, and adaptation strategies for chest X-ray and CT models.

Current questions

  • How does model performance change from common findings to rare findings?
  • Which evaluations expose underrepresented-class failures that aggregate metrics conceal?
  • How do adaptation, grounding, and calibration behave across datasets with different finding frequencies?
03 · Interpretable & Reliable AI

Interpreting model behaviour under shift

I study representation and explanation behaviour under class imbalance and distribution shift, alongside model robustness, grounding, and calibration in radiology.

Current questions

  • What do interpretation methods reveal about biomedical vision-language models under imbalance?
  • How stable are explanations and representations when data shift between settings?
  • How can evaluation distinguish plausible explanations from reliable model behaviour?
