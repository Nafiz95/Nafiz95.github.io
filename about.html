About me

A researcher working at the intersection of medical imaging, multimodal machine learning, and trustworthy evaluation.

I am a PhD candidate in the School of Computing at Queen's University and a researcher in the Big Data Analytics and Management Lab. My current work focuses on medical vision-language models, particularly interpretability, long-tailed learning, and evaluation in radiology.

My earlier work spans biometrics, cybersecurity, reinforcement learning, NLP, clinical data analysis, information retrieval, and visualization. These experiences shaped my interest in AI systems that need to work reliably outside ideal benchmark conditions.
